Google Analytics 4 (GA4) setup

1. Create a Google Analytics 4 property in Google Analytics.
2. Create a Web data stream for your invitation website.
3. Copy the Measurement ID (format: G-XXXXXXXXXX).
4. Open index.html.
5. Replace BOTH occurrences of:
   G-8TZML0ZF41
   with your real Measurement ID.
6. Deploy the website.
7. Open the invitation once yourself and check Google Analytics > Reports > Realtime.

Tracked automatically:
- page_view
- user/session activity
- device category (mobile/tablet/desktop)
- browser and operating system
- approximate geographic location
- traffic source/referrer
- engagement/time on page

Custom invitation events:
- view_invitation — when "View Invitation" is tapped
- add_to_calendar — when "Add to Calendar" is tapped

Important:
GA4 is intended for aggregate analytics. It does not automatically give you a
visitor's name, phone number, email address, or exact identity. Do not send
personally identifiable information to GA4.

If you share this invitation publicly, consider adding an appropriate privacy/
consent notice depending on your audience and applicable privacy requirements.
